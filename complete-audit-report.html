How to read the arrows

Audio flows left→right through one long-lived TaskManager coroutine per call. Every box can wedge that coroutine: S2 drops/double-acks the socket, S4 stalls the transcript, S3 orphans history or re-speaks on WS fallback, S5 overwrites the sender or double-EOS, S6 leaks the tool payload, S7 lets an unauthed dial in, S8 lets it regress. Fix order follows the arrows: authenticate (S2/S7) → survive (S1) → stop the bleed (S6) → harden providers (S3–S5).
